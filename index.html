Welcome to BorschToGo

We are a UK-based community interest company dedicated to post-conflict reconstruction in Ukraine and worldwide. We deliver our mission by harnessing Ukrainian food for global good. Through the universal language of food, culture and shared tables, we bring people together to help rebuild lives and communities. From a simple bowl of borsch to strategic global partnerships, every connection we make helps turn compassion into sustainable impact.

We are grateful for your visit to our site. We look forward to working with you to deliver real, sustainable impacts. On these pages, you will find many ways to connect with us. A very warm welcome awaits.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to post-conflict reconstruction in Ukraine and in communities affected by conflict around the world. Our mission is rooted in a simple but powerful idea: food brings us together. We harness the cultural, social, and economic power of Ukrainian food to create pathways for recovery, resilience, and long-term rebuilding. Through shared meals, cultural exchange, and collaborative initiatives, we transform moments of connection into meaningful support for people rebuilding their lives. SAM CONCEPT · 03 / 17

Our Approach

Explore Our Partnership Opportunities We work at the intersection of humanitarian action and culture, guided by the belief that meaningful recovery is built not only through aid, but through connection. Culture — and especially food — has a unique ability to bring people together across borders, languages, and lived experiences. It creates shared spaces for dialogue, empathy, and trust, transforming awareness into sustained solidarity. By elevating Ukrainian culinary heritage and cultural expression, we invite global audiences to engage with Ukraine beyond the headlines — through its identity, creativity, and resilience. From intimate community gatherings to international collaborations, our initiatives use shared experiences to inspire generosity and mobilise long- term support. Our fundraising model is participatory and partnership-driven. We collaborate with chefs, cultural institutions, businesses, grassroots organisations, and international supporters to design initiatives that are inclusive, scalable, and impact-focused. Each project is developed with care to ensure funds raised translate into tangible, sustainable outcomes. We are committed to long-term recovery. We support projects that restore livelihoods, rebuild social infrastructure, and strengthen communities, ensuring that recovery efforts are locally informed and culturally grounded. Through cultural diplomacy and responsible fundraising, we aim to convert global solidarity into meaningful, lasting impact for those who need it most. Borsch Foundation

Governance

As a registered UK Community Interest Company, our governance model balances strategic oversight with operational flexibility, enabling us to work closely with donors, partners, and local communities to respond to urgent needs while investing in long-term recovery. Every decision is made with accountability, transparency and care, enabling our supporters to see how their generosity transforms into tangible results. We continuously review our policies and procedures to ensure a robust governance and operational framework delivering measurable impact. Our board of trustees provides strategic oversight and ensures adherence to regulatory standards, while operational leadership facilitates flexible, scalable programme delivery. This structure supports cross-border collaboration with partners, donors, and communities in the U.K., Europe, the Middle East, and the United States. We are fully committed to open and efficient resource mobilisation, and evidence-based decision-making. Whilst our overall governance model is designed to maintain accountability while fostering innovation, responsiveness in post-conflict recovery remains our key priority. Borsch Wednesdays

Borsch Wednesdays, our innovative and collaborative partnership programme, aims to engage over 100,000 restaurants worldwide. Participating venues serve Ukrainian borsch on Wednesdays, as part of their chef’s special offering. Diners come to expect borsch as a charitable giving opportunity, while restaurants also make contributions to our reconstruction projects. We envision Borsch Wednesdays becoming a recognisable global brand and movement — shared moments each week when the world comes together over a bowl of borsch for good. BorschCard

BorschCard is our proposed partnership with leading global financial institutions to deliver a purpose-driven payment card for one million customers worldwide. Each transaction generates a small contribution to our fundraising goals, transforming everyday spending into everyday impact. BorschCard holders become part of a global community supporting post-conflict recovery worldwide — without changing their spending habits. With BorschCard, we are working towards creating sustainable and scalable sources of funding. Explore BorschCard BorschCard Transforming everyday spending into everyday impact. Borsch Foundation
