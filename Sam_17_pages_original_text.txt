    Welcome to BorschToGo


     We are a UK-based community interest company dedicated to post-conﬂict
        reconstruction in Ukraine and worldwide. We deliver our mission by harnessing
        Ukrainian food for global good. Through the universal language of food, culture
      and shared tables, we bring people together to help rebuild lives and
       communities. From a simple bowl of borsch to strategic global partnerships,
       every connection we make helps turn compassion into sustainable impact.


     We are grateful for your visit to our site. We look forward to working with you to
        deliver real, sustainable impacts. On these pages, you will ﬁnd many ways to
       connect with us. A very warm welcome awaits.

Borsch

  Governance





      As a registered UK Community Interest Company, our governance model
      balances strategic oversight with operational ﬂexibility, enabling us to work
       closely with donors, partners, and local communities to respond to urgent
      needs while investing in long-term recovery.


       Every decision is made with accountability, transparency and care, enabling
       our supporters to see how their generosity transforms into tangible results.


     We continuously review our policies and procedures to ensure a robust
      governance and operational framework delivering measurable impact. Our
      board of trustees provides strategic oversight and ensures adherence to
       regulatory standards, while operational leadership facilitates ﬂexible,
       scalable programme delivery.


       This structure supports cross-border collaboration with partners, donors,
      and communities in the U.K., Europe, the Middle East, and the United
       States. We are fully committed to open and efﬁcient resource mobilisation,
      and evidence-based decision-making. Whilst our overall governance model
         is designed to maintain accountability while fostering innovation,
       responsiveness in post-conﬂict recovery remains our key priority.

GoBorsch





GoBorsch is a gastro events project.
GoBorsch events are cross-cultural
gatherings where participants cook, eat,
and exchange ideas about promoting
global peace and co-operation. We are
developing a project framework to
deliver GoBorsch sustainably
worldwide, in partnership with selected
local organisations.

Borsch
Wednesdays





Borsch Wednesdays, our innovative
and collaborative partnership
programme, aims to engage over
100,000 restaurants worldwide.
Participating venues serve Ukrainian
borsch on Wednesdays, as part of
their chef’s special oﬀering.

Diners come to expect borsch as a
charitable giving opportunity, while
restaurants also make contributions to
our reconstruction projects. We
envision Borsch Wednesdays
becoming a recognisable global brand
and movement — shared moments
each week when the world comes
together over a bowl of borsch for
good.

BorschCard

 Transforming everyday spending
 into everyday impact.





BorschCard is our proposed partnership
with leading global ﬁnancial institutions
to deliver a purpose-driven payment
card for one million customers
worldwide. Each transaction generates a
small contribution to our fundraising
goals, transforming everyday spending
into everyday impact. BorschCard
holders become part of a global
community supporting post-conﬂict
recovery worldwide — without changing
their spending habits. With BorschCard,
we are working towards creating
sustainable and scalable sources of
funding.
